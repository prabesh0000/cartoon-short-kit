# Sensitive topics: rules Claude Code must follow

Read this whenever a reel touches **tobacco, alcohol, drugs, health or medicine, news about real people, religion and legends, ethnic or caste identity, or politics.** These rules override style choices. If a rule blocks something the brief asks for, stop and tell the user instead of guessing.

## 1. Tobacco, alcohol, vapes, drugs
- **Never show or say a brand:** no names, logos, pack colours or pack shapes that identify a real product. Use a plain white pack and filter. Nepal's Tobacco Products (Control and Regulation) Act 2011, Section 10, bans tobacco promotion in any media, the internet included.
- **Never make it look good:** no cool smoke rings, no slow-motion exhales, no "relaxed" or "stylish" smoker, no smoking set to upbeat music. Smoke is grey, heavy and slightly ugly.
- **No one who looks under 18 holds or uses the product.** Adult bean-heads only (taller, adult faces, adult clothes).
- **Educational framing from the first second:** myth vs fact, health facts, history. TikTok makes content that promotes tobacco ineligible for the For You feed and allows educational exceptions, so the educational purpose must be obvious.
- **No how-to:** never show how to use, crush, roll, buy or hide a product.
- **Facts only from health bodies or peer-reviewed research** (WHO, national health ministries, journals), cited in the script file.
- **Don't fear-monger past the evidence:** say "risk rises" or "can", never "will" or "always", unless the source says so.

## 2. Health and medical claims
- Every number needs a source in the script file. Round numbers as the source does ("चालीस प्रतिशत").
- Use the source's own certainty: "सक्छ" (may), "सम्भावना" (chance), "खतरा बढ्छ" (risk rises).
- No dosages, treatments or "cures". No advice that replaces a doctor.
- Don't show graphic medical imagery (organs, blood, surgery). Use symbols: a cradle, an empty chair, a bar chart.

## 3. News about real, living people
- Narration keeps "आरोप छ" (it is alleged) and "दोष प्रमाणित भएको छैन" (guilt is not proven).
- 2+ independent sources per claim, dated.
- No real names of officials or judges on screen; no caricatures that mock a real person. Use faceless silhouettes.
- Re-check the facts on the day you render.

## 4. Religion, legends and deities
- Deities are shown from behind, as a glow, or in their worshipped form, and are never mocked or made comic.
- Never call a legend "fake" or "false". Use "भनिन्छ" (it is said) or "लोककथा अनुसार" (according to folklore), and keep legend and history clearly separate.
- If a legend has several versions (Yalambar / Barbarik), say which version this is: "Kathmandu's version".

## 5. Ethnic, caste and community identity
- Don't tell one community's story through another community's lens without saying so (the Yalambar comments: Kirati viewers objected to the Hindu-epic framing).
- No stereotyped looks, accents or costumes. Clothing must be respectful and accurate.
- Never mention caste unless the story is about caste and the user approved it.

## 6. Politics
- No opinions, no party symbols, no tagging politicians.

## 7. Before rendering a sensitive reel: checklist
- [ ] No brand, logo or identifiable pack anywhere
- [ ] Nothing makes the product, act or person look appealing or comic in a harmful way
- [ ] Every number and claim matches the sources in the script file
- [ ] Hedging words kept ("सक्छ", "आरोप", "भनिन्छ")
- [ ] No minors near tobacco, alcohol or drugs
- [ ] Caption and hashtags are educational, with no product or brand hashtags
- [ ] For TikTok: tell the user to run TikTok Studio's content check before posting
