## Rules for this reel
- The vulture explanation is the leading theory, not proven. Narration says "The best explanation?" Keep that; never put "PROVEN" or "SOLVED" on screen.
- Meat is cartoon meat: pink-red chunks with a marbled fat stripe, no gore, no bones, no blood splats.
- Mrs. Crouch and the two tasters are generic 1876 farm bean-heads (bonnet + apron; work shirts, suspenders, hats). The tasters' faces: chew → squint → shrug.
- Vultures are cartoon birds, funny not scary. The vomit is shown as a cartoon "blegh" with chunks, not a realistic stream.
- Facts are fixed: March 1876, Bath County Kentucky, several minutes, two men tasted it (lamb or deer). Don't add numbers.

## Narration (record exactly this)
```
What would you do if chunks of meat started falling from the sky? Because on a clear March morning in eighteen seventy-six, that's exactly what happened to Mrs. Crouch, making soap in her Kentucky yard. For several minutes it poured meat, and two men tasted it, guessing lamb, maybe deer. But nobody looked high enough. The best explanation? A flock of vultures, because when one vulture throws up, the rest join in. So… what would you do?
```
Recording: energetic fact-video narrator. Curious and quick on the opening question, slightly hushed on "nobody looked high enough", a beat before "A flock of vultures", deadpan straight-to-camera on the final question. No long pauses.
