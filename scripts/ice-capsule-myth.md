# आइस चुरोटको बल (ice cigarette capsule myth): health myth-buster

Status: script final, waiting for the voiceover (`inbox/ice-capsule-myth.mp3`). Length: about 64 words ≈ 32 s.
Emotion: curiosity → unease. Twist: the fertility risk is not only in the capsule, it is the whole cigarette; the cool capsule makes smoke feel lighter, so people may inhale deeper.
**Loop ending:** "…ट्याग गरेर सोध्नुहोस् कि…" runs straight into the first line ("के आइस चुरोट…?") on replay, so the hook becomes the question you ask your friend. Trim all silence at the start and end of the voiceover so the loop has no gap.

## Rules for this reel (tobacco)
- **Never show or say a brand** (no Shikhar/Surya names, logos, pack designs) on screen or in the caption. Nepal's Tobacco Products (Control and Regulation) Act 2011, Section 10, bans tobacco promotion in any media, online included. Use a plain, unbranded white pack and filter.
- No glamorous smoking: smoke is grey and heavy, never cool or stylish. No characters enjoying a cigarette.
- Health-facts framing only.

## Hook layers (0:00)
- **First frame:** close-up of fingers squeezing a plain filter; the small capsule inside cracks with a "pop" (mid-action). A rumour speech bubble behind: `बच्चा हुँदैन रे!`
- **Spoken line:** line 1.
- **Overlay:** `सत्य झन् डरलाग्दो छ`
- **Beat overlays:** `बल मात्र होइन` (line 3), `४०% बढी खतरा` (line 4).

## Narration (record exactly this)
```
के आइस चुरोट पट्काएर खाँदा, साँच्चिकै पछि बच्चा हुँदैन?
फिल्टरभित्रको त्यो सानो बलमा मेन्थोल हुन्छ, जसले धुवाँलाई चिसो र हल्का बनाउँछ।
तर अचम्मको कुरा, बच्चा नहुने खतरा बलमा मात्र होइन, पूरै चुरोटमा छ।
डब्लुएचओका अनुसार, चुरोट खाने महिलामा बच्चा नहुने खतरा चालीस प्रतिशत बढी हुन्छ,
र पुरुषको पनि सन्तान हुने सम्भावना घट्छ।
अनि त्यो चिसो बलले धुवाँ हल्का लगाउँछ, त्यसैले मान्छे झन् गहिरो तान्न सक्छन्।
आइस पट्काउने आफ्नो साथीलाई ट्याग गरेर सोध्नुहोस् कि...
```
Recording: "डब्लुएचओ" must be read as the letters W-H-O (test that line). Let "कि…" rise like an unfinished sentence. No pause at the very end.

## Sources (checked 18 Nov 2026)
- WHO review (Sep 2026): women who smoke have a 40% higher risk of infertility; in men smoking is linked to poorer semen quality and sexual dysfunction: https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/smoking-could-be-lowering-fertility-for-both-men-and-women
- Capsules add menthol/fruit flavour when crushed and reduce harshness: https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/campus/university-of-otago/young-people-vulnerable-flavoured-cigarette-capsules
- Menthol may encourage deeper and longer inhalation: https://exposetobacco.org/wp-content/uploads/Flavors-Including-Menthol-In-Tobacco-Products-FINAL.pdf
- Capsule chemicals include toxic, potentially carcinogenic compounds (so the ball is not "harmless"): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11033577
- No source found claiming the capsule alone causes infertility (the myth).
