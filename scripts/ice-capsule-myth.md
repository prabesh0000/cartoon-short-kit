# आइस चुरोटको बल (ice cigarette capsule myth): health myth-buster

Status: script final, voiceover `inbox/ice-capsule-myth.mp3`. Length: about 63 words ≈ 31 s.
Emotion: curiosity → unease. Twist: the fertility risk is not only in the capsule, it is the whole cigarette, and the cool capsule makes smoke feel lighter, so people may inhale deeper.
Loop ending: yes. "…ट्याग गरेर सोध्नुहोस् कि…" runs straight into line 1 ("के आइस चुरोट…?") on replay.
Sensitive topic: **tobacco + health.** Follow `guides/sensitive-topics.md` sections 1, 2 and 7.

## Rules for this reel
- **No brand anywhere:** never Shikhar, Surya or any real pack; plain white pack and filter only. Not in the caption either.
- **Educational from frame 0:** the rumour bubble and "myth vs fact" framing make the purpose obvious (TikTok exempts educational content but restricts content that promotes tobacco).
- No one enjoys a cigarette on screen. The only person inhaling (line 6) is shown as a simple diagram, not a mood shot.
- Adults only: no young-looking bean-heads near cigarettes.
- No medical gore: infertility is shown with symbols (a rocking cradle that stops, a rising bar).

## Hook layers (0:00)
- **First frame:** extreme close-up: fingers squeeze a plain filter and the capsule cracks with a visible "pop" flash (mid-action, works on mute).
- **Spoken line:** line 1.
- **Overlay:** `सत्य झन् डरलाग्दो छ` (from 0:00 to the end of line 1)
- **Beat overlays:** `बल मात्र होइन` (line 3), `४०% बढी खतरा` (line 4, next to the bar), `गहिरो तान` (line 6, small, near the diagram)

## Narration (record exactly this)
```
के आइस चुरोट पट्काएर खाँदा, साँच्चिकै पछि बच्चा हुँदैन?
फिल्टरभित्रको त्यो सानो बलमा मेन्थोल हुन्छ, जसले धुवाँलाई चिसो र हल्का बनाउँछ।
तर अचम्मको कुरा, बच्चा नहुने खतरा बलमा मात्र होइन, पूरै चुरोटमा छ।
डब्लुएचओका अनुसार, चुरोट खाने महिलामा बच्चा नहुने खतरा चालीस प्रतिशत बढी हुन्छ,
र पुरुषको पनि सन्तान हुने सम्भावना घट्छ।
अनि त्यो चिसो बलले धुवाँ हल्का लगाउँछ, त्यसैले मान्छे झन् गहिरो तान्न सक्छन्।
आइस पट्काउने आफ्नो साथीलाई ट्याग गरेर सोध्नुहोस् कि...
```
Recording: dramatic news-anchor tone. "डब्लुएचओ" must be read as the letters W-H-O (test that line). Let "कि…" rise like an unfinished sentence. Trim all silence at the start and end of the file.

## Sources (checked 18 Nov 2026)
- WHO review (Sep 2026): women who smoke have a 40% higher risk of infertility; in men smoking is linked to poorer semen quality and sexual dysfunction: https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/smoking-could-be-lowering-fertility-for-both-men-and-women
- Capsules add menthol or fruit flavour when crushed and reduce harshness: https://www.odt.co.nz/news/dunedin/campus/university-of-otago/young-people-vulnerable-flavoured-cigarette-capsules
- Menthol may encourage deeper and longer inhalation: https://exposetobacco.org/wp-content/uploads/Flavors-Including-Menthol-In-Tobacco-Products-FINAL.pdf
- Capsule chemicals include toxic, potentially carcinogenic compounds (so the ball is not "harmless"): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11033577
- No source found claiming the capsule alone causes infertility (the myth).
