## Rules for this reel
- **Nobody smokes on screen.** No joints, chillums, pipes, smoke, rolling, buying or hiding. No close-up or hero shot of a cannabis leaf; the topic is carried by the narration and the text.
- **Educational from frame 0:** the rumour bubble and the "science vs chowk gossip" framing make the purpose obvious.
- **Adults only.** Every bean-head is clearly adult. The young man being measured in shot 1 is an adult (adult face and height, adult clothes).
- **The uncle (काका)** is an original character: a chowk uncle with a tea glass, confident and a bit cocky. He never uses anything, and he is never shown as high. Respectful clothing, no ethnic or caste stereotype. He is the butt of the joke only for believing a rumour.
- **Body shown with taste:** hips and butt shown through clothes only (jeans, a measuring tape), never a bare body. The fat diagram (line 7) is a clean textbook outline, no realistic anatomy.
- **No numbers beyond the sources:** "about one kilo" (the study's ~2 lb example), "33,000 people", "3 years". Do not write "weed makes you thin".

## Sources (checked 19 Nov 2026)
- 3-year prospective study (NESARC, n = 33,000, ages 18+): all participants gained weight; cannabis users gained less (lower BMI increase): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30879064/ (full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6857742)
- The difference was modest, about 2 lb (~1 kg) for a 5'7", ~200 lb participant; cause still unknown: https://neurosciencenews.com/weight-marijuana-12023/
- Observational, cannot infer causality; the lead author warns against using cannabis to lose weight: https://sechat.com.br/noticia/cannabis-users-are-less-likely-to-be-overweight-or-obese
- Cannabis increases appetite ("munchies"), yet users tend to weigh less: https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/325050.php
- Spot reduction: six weeks of daily ab exercise (diet held constant) did not reduce abdominal fat (Vispute et al. 2011, J Strength Cond Res): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21804427/ ; "the human body does not pull fat from specific areas" (NSCA): https://www.nsca.com/contentassets/60c4deb55cd640549806ed8f3f170b9e/trainertips_spot-reduction.pdf
- No source found claiming cannabis takes fat from the buttocks specifically (the myth).
- Nepal: sale, cultivation and consumption are banned under the Narcotic Drugs (Control) Act 1976: https://thehimalayantimes.com/kathmandu/bill-to-legalise-marijuana-cultivation-and-sale-registered
