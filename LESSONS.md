## 2026-11-25 — "The Serial Killer Who Never Existed" (English YouTube fact, real-case news; 45.1 s = 44.31 s voice + 0.8 s hold, 1353 frames, 16 shots; voice `inbox/killer.wav`)
- Brief expected `phantom-dna.mp3`; the user saved it as `killer.wav`. faster-whisper (en) matched the brief word for word ("touched him" = "them"). Facts re-checked on render day (40 crimes / six murders per ISO; UPI says 39 — the brief's 40 kept; €300,000 in Jan 2009).
- Built from `reels/test-height` (same engine) with a new `web/phantom.js`: gloved hand + sealed swab tube, an evidence board (40 slots on 3 ellipse rings, strings with per-card slack stagger, six murder cards tinted, year + count tags, faceless female silhouette with a "?" that drops), a schematic Germany map (+ France/Austria edges, pins, all states lit except the SE one, DIFFERENT SUPPLIER label), a DNA profile printout (flat on the desk), a reward poster with a blank face, an application form whose fingerprint resolves into DNA bands, an evidence kit of STERILE packets, single packets on a conveyor, a forensic-standard page with a 2016 stamp, `drawSilBackK` (faceless detective from BEHIND: no shirt V), and the factory worker (white coat, hairnet, **surgical mask + lowered eyes via `back: true`**, blue gloves) — anonymous even when the head is in frame.
- `camAt().roll` is in DEGREES (main.js converts). Pen `poly(pts, fill, ol)` and `ell(..., fill, ol, a0, a1, rot)` take an outline WIDTH only (ink colour); for coloured outlines use `stroke([...pts, pts[0]], w, col)`.
- The text planner protects the whole board panel area via proxies: a protected card blurred far behind the hook (the suspect) still blocks the top slot — drop that proxy during the hook.
- Frame-diff: an `ease: "out"` push on the string-covered board (8.6x), a 2x push into the kit (10x) and a full-speed follow of a conveyor packet (9x) were all too fast; linear, shorter pushes and a camera that travels HALF the packet's distance (the packet drifts across the frame) fixed them. A pose that switches in one frame (worker reach) spikes — blend it over ~10 frames.
- Sound "cold forensic thriller": low D strings, a pulse from the second card, ten held low notes entering one by one as the board fills, tremolo + riser into the fingerprint, everything cut dead on "Impossible" (sub hit + room tone), a bright F-major pluck/marimba loop + conveyor hum in the factory, a low drone to the end. All 24 cues OK, music -6.0, SFX -11.9, speech-band median -17.2 dB. Loudness came out at **-15.5 LUFS**: this narrator's take is quiet (mix_raw -22.1 LUFS, peaks near 0 dBFS), so the +8.1 dB gain drove the limiter; consider `LUFS=-16` for high-crest voices.
- Deviations from the brief: the van → kit → scene → tweezers handoff is shortened to the conveyor → sealer ride; €300,000 is printed on the poster instead of an overlay.
- **v2 (user: "make the woman look like a killer, detective hats, make it more real, improve what you can")**: the imagined suspect became a hooded figure (`hoodedK`: pointed hood, face lost in black shadow, collar up, two faint eye glints, no weapon) on the board and the poster; at "no killer" she fades to a dashed empty outline as the "?" falls (= never existed). Kept strictly apart from the real factory worker's look (white coat, hairnet, mask). Detectives now wear fedoras + belted trench coats with a faint cool rim light (`fedoraK`, `trenchK`, front `drawDetK` with a purple glove, back `drawSilBackK`) — they finally read against the dark office. Also: year/count tags moved inside the medium-shot width, a numbered evidence-marker tent at the doorway, a bigger swab, and IMPOSSIBLE as a large overlay (`"size": 104`, `"y": 300`) clear of the card's protected area.

A ghost needs real transparency + glow outline + float. Hanging signs need something to hang from. The hook jump needs anticipation + clear air + an out-of-reach prop. 8 panel shots in a row (~18 s) feel like a slideshow — keep a character in the fact shots. Flores read muddy (big beige rock, flat grass): layer foliage, add mist/rays.
